\chapter{Future Work}\label{chapter:future-work}

This chapter discusses directions for extending the work presented in this thesis.

\section{Progressive Sequence Length Training}

The current system trains exclusively on 4-frame windows ($\text{max\_ahead}=3$), while AnyCam's original training progressively increases from 2-frame to 8-frame sequences. This difference likely contributes to our model's ATE win rate of 57\% on Sintel---competitive but not dominant. Training with progressively longer sequences would expose the model to longer-range temporal dependencies and could improve trajectory-level accuracy. This is a concrete and immediate next step.

\section{Scaling to Longer Sequences}

Beyond progressive training, the fixed 4-frame window limits the temporal context available for both calibration aggregation and pose consistency. Possible extensions include:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Hierarchical flow composition}: Composing flows in a tree structure to limit composition depth to $O(\log N)$ instead of $O(N)$.
    \item \textbf{Recurrent or streaming architectures}: Replacing fixed-length MCT aggregation with a recurrent module that maintains state across frames, enabling arbitrarily long sequences.
    \item \textbf{Keyframe selection}: Identifying the most informative frames for aggregation rather than using consecutive frames uniformly.
\end{itemize}

\section{Broader Training Data Distribution}

The KITTI calibration limitation (Section~\ref{sec:kitti-limitation}) demonstrates that the MCT does not generalise to camera types underrepresented in training. The current training data (indoor walkthroughs, urban walking, YouTube content) lacks automotive driving scenarios with long focal lengths and forward-facing motion. Extending the training distribution to include driving datasets would likely address this domain gap.

\section{Non-Pinhole Camera Models}

Although AnyCalib supports model-agnostic calibration through its per-pixel ray representation, this thesis fits a pinhole model to the predicted rays for compatibility with AnyCam's projection-based pose estimation. Extending to non-pinhole models (fisheye, equirectangular) would require modifying the flow induction to operate on ray fields directly, fully exploiting AnyCalib's capabilities.

\section{Joint Depth Refinement}

Depth predictions from UniDepth are treated as fixed inputs. Since depth and calibration are coupled through the projection equations, joint refinement of depth, calibration, and pose could yield further improvements, particularly where monocular depth estimates are unreliable.
